About the school

A school built on learning, values and leadership

Who they are

Kautilya Vidyalaya is a higher secondary school in Mysuru (Mysore), Karnataka. It runs from kindergarten up to pre-university level and is managed by the J. Ranganna Lakshmamma Charitable Trust.

What they aim for

The school focuses on academic results alongside sports, creative activities and character building, so that students develop modern skills, ethics and leadership.

Where they are

The school is located in the Kanakadasa Nagar and Dattagalli area of Mysuru, a residential part of the city.

Campus life

Facilities, one by one

Each part of the campus, what it is used for, and why it matters to students.

Main Campus & Architecture

The main campus is designed around classrooms, open spaces and walkways. Students move between classes, labs and the sports grounds on one site, which keeps the day organised and easy to follow for younger children.

Swimming Pool

The school has its own swimming pool. It is used for swimming lessons, which encourage water safety, fitness and confidence. Lessons are run under trained instructors with a focus on safety.

Playground & Sports

Physical education is part of the daily routine. The grounds are used for football, cricket, basketball and track-and-field events, which build teamwork, discipline and a competitive spirit.

Atal Tinkering Lab

Set up under the Atal Innovation Mission by NITI Aayog, the lab is a hands-on workspace for electronics, robotics, 3D printing and Internet of Things projects. Students are encouraged to build and test their own ideas.

Auditorium & Events

The school hosts assemblies, annual days, investiture ceremonies and cultural programmes. These events give students a stage for performance, public speaking and leadership roles.

Classrooms

Classrooms are used for CBSE subjects from kindergarten to pre-university. Teaching combines the board syllabus with practical activities and projects.

Library

The library gives students a quiet place to read, research and prepare for exams. It supports both school textbooks and general reading for curiosity.

Canteen

The school has a canteen where students can get food and snacks during the day. It is a place for a break between classes and sports sessions.
